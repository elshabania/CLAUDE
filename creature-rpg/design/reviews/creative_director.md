# Creative Director review: cross-document conflicts (round 1)

Reviewer: Creative Director. Scope: how `creatures.md`, `systems.md`, `world.md`, `rendering_and_architecture.md`, `qa_plan.md` and `release_character_gate.md` affect narrative, character language, Resonance, UI and audio as set in `creative_direction.md` (CD v1).
Method: skimmed each document for the areas above and read the conflicting sections closely. Nothing was built or tested; all findings come from reading the documents.

Severity:
- **Blocker**: the documents cannot both be implemented; data validation or the story graph would fail.
- **Major**: player-visible inconsistency, or a gate check measuring against the wrong spec.
- **Minor**: cosmetic, or one document just needs a note.

## 0. Guiding decision

`world.md` already fixes geometry, gate coordinates, encounter-based softlock proofs, trainer tables and the quest graph. `systems.md` owns the math, AI and item data.

**My preferred overall resolution:**
- CD v2 adopts world's topology, trial order, gate list and flag/quest ids.
- CD v2 adopts systems' numbers and item ids.
- The CD keeps ownership of all **display names, story beats, dialogue, character language, UI and audio**. world.md labels its own names as working names, so this matches its intent.

After this round I will revise `creative_direction.md` to match (listed in §3). I have not edited any other file.
