# GAME BIBLE — *Wildchord: Songs of Cantarra*

Authoritative creative and gameplay specification (orchestrator, design gate v2, 2026-09-24). This bible states the canon in one place and points to the owning document for detail. Precedence: **DECISIONS.md > this bible > owner documents (v2)**. Playtimes are estimates; nothing here has been playtested.

## 1. Pitch and identity
An original single-player 3D creature-collecting RPG in the browser. In the highland region of **Cantarra**, standing **Chordstones** hum in answer to creatures called **kin**. The player, a newly licensed **Tuner** (default name **Hollis**, pronouns chosen), earns six **Keynotes** from the **Cantors** of six **Cadence Halls**, stops **the Stillmark** (a survey guild led by **Magister Odile Graven** that fits Stillbells over the stones to control when they sound), and sounds the Great Chord at **Concord Spire** against **the Concordant, Rhea Rookwell**, mother of the rival **Cass Rookwell**. Tone: warm, sincere, lightly comic, real stakes; kin never die, they "go quiet". Detail: creative_direction.md v2.

Terminology (never use franchise terms): kin · Tuner · troupe (party ≤ 6) · the Fosterage (storage ≤ 300) · Cadence Hall / Cantor / Keynote · Chimes (Reed, Brass, Silver, Crown) · the Kinsong (encyclopedia; seen = "heard", caught = "sung") · Etudes (reusable teaching discs) · Hearthrest (healing) · Chandlery (shop) · tallies ◇ (currency) · Crescendo (evolution) · Waystones (fast travel) · Resonance (signature mechanic) · status names Scorch/Blight/Jolt/Drowse/Rimebite, volatile Muddled · effectiveness text "Resounding!", "Muffled…", "No echo.".

## 2. Signature mechanic — Resonance
- **Field:** landmarks carved with a type glyph are Resonance nodes. A troupe member of that type (fainted or not) transforms them permanently. Ten registers: Rootcall (verdant), Spark (electric), Kindle (fire), Swell (water), Heave (stone), Seep (toxin), Gust (gale), Veil (shade), Rime (frost), Gleam (lumen). Tutorial unlocks Rootcall + the three starter registers; Keynotes 1–6 unlock Heave, Seep, Gust, Veil, Rime, Gleam. Only four nodes are mandatory (forest Rootgate, cave lower galleries, route_4 ascent, snowpeak falls), each with a Resonance Steward fallback. Every other node gates secrets only.
- **Battle:** each outdoor zone has an attuned type; damaging moves of that type get ×11/10 after same-type bonus, for both sides (×6/5 with `tr_resonant`). Silenced zones (route_3 before ch5, route_5 before ch11, Odile phase A) give no bonus. HUD pill shows glyph + text.
- **Fast travel:** each zone's Chordstone is a Waystone registered by resonating once.

## 3. World and campaign (world.md v2, creative_direction §4)
Zone order: Larkhollow (town_1) → Thistledown Way (route_1, lumen) → Murmurwood (forest, verdant, **trial_1 Wren, verdant**) → Brackenridge Pass (route_2, stone) → Knellstone (town_2, **trial_2 Dorran Shale, stone**) → The Undertone (cave, electric) → Sallowfen (route_3, toxin) → Sillowmere (lake, water, **trial_3 Nerys, water**) → Galewick (town_3, **trial_4 Tamsin, gale**) → Highscar Rise (route_4, gale; Stillhouse interior) → Mount Cindral (volcano, fire, **trial_5 Bastian, fire**) → Gloamstair (route_5, shade) → Hoarcrown (snowpeak, frost, **trial_6 Isaure, frost**; Great Stillbell summit, Odile) → Concord Spire (league: Rival 6, Concordant Rhea). 12 chapters, critical path est. ≈ 9 h 20 m (estimate).
Story battles: 17 (systems §14.2) — 6 rival (rival takes the starter strong against the player's: Water > Fire > Electric > Water), 6 Cantors, Brann ×1, Vey ×2, Odile two-phase, Concordant. Unchosen starters: leftover starter rescued at the Stillhouse (ch8, `q_foster_leftover`, Lv 25); rival's line via `q_second_clutch` after trial_5 (Lv 30).

## 4. Creatures (creatures.md v2, species.json)
30 kin, 10 three-stage families, one primary type per family and a distinct secondary type from stage 2 or 3: f01 Fizzkit→Crackleap→Tempestrel (electric; →gale) · f02 Wickwool→Kilnhorn→Smoulderam (fire; →gale — ram-horned dragon line, D31) · f03 Rippleback→Tidesleek→Floeguard (water; →frost) · f04 Dozebud→Lullstalk→Belladrowse (verdant·toxin) · f05 Rollith→Cairnback→Lodestodon (stone; →electric) · f06 Rimelet→Sleetribbon→Borealoop (frost; →lumen) · f07 Gustling→Whirlseed→Samarch (gale·verdant) · f08 Ringdrip→Brineloop→Drapetide (toxin·water) · f09 Snipling→Marionyx→Emberfold (shade; →fire) · f10 Dawnfry→Lumarlin→Coronaleen (lumen; →shade). Every stage changes body plan; each species is a bespoke procedural builder (`src/creatures/species/cXX.ts`) with 8-state painted faces and tag-driven procedural animation.

## 5. Systems (systems.md v2)
Integer, seeded, deterministic battle simulation: stats from base/potential/level/temperament; damage steps (weather → crit → random → STAB → Resonance ×11/10 → effectiveness → burn/frostbite → traits); 10×10 matrix (28 strong / 27 resisted / 2 immune); priority brackets; 5 majors + volatiles; weather (clear/rain/snow/fog/sunlight); 101 moves (m000 fallback + 10 per type); 26 traits, one per species; capture a-value with four Chime tiers and up to three rings; XP with shared lessons (non-participants 50 %); level curves fast/medium/slow; evolution by level (f10 stage 3 also by Dawn Prism); AI easy/normal/hard on an information-limited view with its own RNG stream; economy that never needs grinding; anti-softlock rules (wipe → last Hearthrest with capped tally penalty, free Chimes when broke, Scramble when out of charges, weariness ends stalls by turn 65).

## 6. Character language (creative_direction §5, release_character_gate)
Appealing, expressive, silhouette-first. Big readable eyes (painted atlas: open, half, closed, happy, hurt, faint, determined, surprised), key light upper-left, rim light always on, 2–3 dominant colours per kin, type colour as accent, one hero feature per family plus a stage-2 silhouette break. Humans are stylised (4.75 heads teen) with a silhouette hook each. Originality: no recognisable imitation; names screened for similarity (release agent). The Release & Character Consistency Agent issues pass/conditional/fail per phase.

## 7. Presentation (rendering_and_architecture v2)
Vite + React 19 + TypeScript 7 + R3F 9 + drei + Rapier + postprocessing + zustand + Tone.js. Discrete zones (~120–220 m) built from data: heightfield terrain with trimesh collider, instanced vegetation with wind, procedural buildings, stylised water, fog, day/night and weather. Battles are staged in the current zone. Quality profiles High / Balanced / Mobile. All assets procedural (asset sites are blocked).

## 8. UI and audio (creative_direction §7–8)
Palette tokens (night, parchment, brass, resonance), fork-notch panels, system font stacks (no network fonts), glyph-coded type chips with 3-letter codes, HP "resonance string" with solid/dashed/dotted bands, keyboard-first menus, reduced motion, text size, camera sensitivity. Tone.js-synthesised zone themes and layered battle music (bar-quantised), per-family cries, synthesised SFX; audio only after a gesture; master/music/sfx volume and mute.

## 9. Persistence
Versioned localStorage envelope with checksum, atomic tmp→verify→backup→main writes, recovery main→tmp→backup, quota and no-storage modes, export/import, new-game confirmation. Only committed state is saved; reload mid-battle returns to the pre-battle checkpoint.
